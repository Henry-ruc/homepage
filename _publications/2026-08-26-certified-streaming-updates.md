We introduce S3, a streaming learning framework that combines online sampling with Newton-step updates to handle limited throughput, with certified approximation guarantees to full retraining.

**Authors:** Dongxie Wen, Lu Yi, Fengtai Li, Shaoqiu Zhang, Xiao Zhang, and Zhewei Wei.

Published online in **IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (TPAMI)** on **26 August 2026** (Early Access, pp. 1–18).
